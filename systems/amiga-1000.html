Preservation scope

What gets recorded.

Preserve Kickstart and Workbench media, document motherboard and expansion details, test video and audio, and record the system’s provenance.

  • Photograph the exterior, labels, ports, internals, and installed options.
  • Document operational state, known faults, repairs, and safe power-up procedure.
  • Inventory ROMs, storage devices, expansion hardware, peripherals, and accessories.
  • Image original media and publish hashes, formats, recovery notes, and usable derivatives.
  • Scan or link manuals, service information, advertisements, and other surviving context.
